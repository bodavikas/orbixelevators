Safety is part of every specification.

Confirm these items for the selected model with the manufacturer and qualified elevator professionals. This checklist is not a claim that every feature is included in the concept.

  • Confirm the traction machine, suspension ropes/belts, governor, safety gear, buffers, guide rails, counterweight and control system are matched and approved for the installation.
  • MRL equipment still requires approved access and safe rescue/maintenance provisions; machine-room-less does not mean maintenance-free.
  • Verify door interlocks, overspeed protection, emergency communication, backup/rescue operation, electrical protection and required inspections with the OEM.
  • Confirm door interlocks, obstruction detection and safe door reopening behaviour for the selected configuration.
  • Confirm overload sensing, brake performance, speed governor and other applicable overspeed protection devices.
  • Confirm emergency alarm/intercom, backup power or automatic rescue arrangements, and the building's emergency procedure.
  • Require commissioning tests, periodic maintenance records and inspection by appropriately qualified lift professionals.

Never use an elevator that is out of service, has damaged doors, unusual movement or an alarm fault. Keep entrances clear, do not exceed the posted rated load, and follow the installed equipment’s instructions. In an emergency, use the alarm/communication system and wait for trained rescue personnel; do not attempt self-rescue or force doors open.

HELPFUL ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

A few starting points before planning a home elevator.

Can an elevator be added to an existing home? +

In many cases it may be possible, but feasibility depends on the building layout, available space, structural conditions and selected system. A site assessment is essential.

Do all home elevators require a pit or machine room? +

Requirements vary by model and OEM. Some systems may offer pitless or machine-room-less configurations; confirm details for the chosen equipment.

How much does a home elevator cost? +

Pricing depends on travel, stops, rated load, finishes, civil work, installation and service arrangements. Contact Orbix for a project-specific discussion.

How long does installation take? +

The timeline depends on site readiness, equipment lead time, civil work, installation and inspection requirements. A confirmed schedule should be agreed with the supplier.

What maintenance is required? +

Maintenance frequency and service coverage depend on the installed equipment and OEM guidance. Confirm the service agreement before purchase.
