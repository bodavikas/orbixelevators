SAFETY, BEFORE EVERYTHING

A safer journey starts with good decisions.

Safety depends on the selected elevator, the building, correct installation and ongoing maintenance. Explore these practical checkpoints before planning, using or servicing a lift.

01 / PASSENGERS↕

Ride responsibly

Let passengers exit before entering. Keep clear of the doors, never force them open, and do not exceed the rated load shown for the installed lift.

Everyday use
02 / CHILDREN＋

Keep children supervised

Children should be accompanied and taught not to play with the controls, doors or landing area. Keep hands, clothing and belongings away from moving doors.

Family awareness
03 / EMERGENCY!

If the lift stops

Stay calm, use the installed alarm or emergency communication method, and contact the authorised rescue or service team. Never try to climb out or pry doors open.

Emergency response
04 / MAINTENANCE⌁

Leave servicing to experts

Arrange inspections and preventive maintenance as specified by the elevator manufacturer and applicable local requirements. Do not bypass interlocks or attempt repairs yourself.

Authorised technicians
05 / INSTALLATION⌂

Confirm site readiness

Before installation, have qualified professionals verify the shaft, pit or pitless arrangement, power supply, structural requirements, access and emergency provisions for the chosen model.

Project planning
06 / SPECIFICATIONS✓

Verify before purchase

Ask for written confirmation of rated capacity, speed, travel, door protection, backup or rescue provisions, applicable certifications and the maintenance agreement for the exact equipment supplied.

Documented assurance
Important: This is general safety guidance, not a substitute for the owner’s manual, a site assessment or applicable regulations. Safety equipment and emergency procedures vary by model and installation; confirm them with the manufacturer, installer and local authority. Do not describe any certification or feature as available until it is verified for the actual product.
HELPFUL ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

A few starting points before planning a home elevator.

Can an elevator be added to an existing home? +

In many cases it may be possible, but feasibility depends on the building layout, available space, structural conditions and selected system. A site assessment is essential.

Do all home elevators require a pit or machine room? +

Requirements vary by model and OEM. Some systems may offer pitless or machine-room-less configurations; confirm details for the chosen equipment.

How much does a home elevator cost? +

Pricing depends on travel, stops, rated load, finishes, civil work, installation and service arrangements. Contact Orbix for a project-specific discussion.

How long does installation take? +

The timeline depends on site readiness, equipment lead time, civil work, installation and inspection requirements. A confirmed schedule should be agreed with the supplier.

What maintenance is required? +

Maintenance frequency and service coverage depend on the installed equipment and OEM guidance. Confirm the service agreement before purchase.
