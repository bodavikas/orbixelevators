02 PROJECT EXECUTION

Safety comes
before progress.

Safety practices are planned around each site, the work being carried out and the applicable requirements. The controls below are a project-stage checklist, not a claim of certification.

SITE SAFETY

Precautions during
ongoing projects.

Swipe or use the arrows to explore key precautions. Site-specific risk assessments and local rules always take priority.

01
SITE MANAGEMENT

Secure work zones

Mark work areas clearly, control access, keep walkways clear and communicate site hazards to everyone involved.

02
SHAFT PROTECTION

Protect openings

Secure lift-shaft openings and edges with suitable barriers or covers; never rely on warning signs alone.

03
PPE

Wear required protection

Use task-appropriate helmets, safety footwear, gloves, eye protection and fall-protection equipment where required.

04
LIFTING & STRUCTURE

Check before lifting

Inspect lifting equipment, approved rigging points, load limits and structural readiness before moving heavy components.

05
ELECTRICAL SAFETY

Isolate energy sources

Only authorised personnel should perform electrical work, using appropriate isolation and lockout procedures.

06
QUALITY CHECKS

Inspect each stage

Record inspections, resolve defects before proceeding and complete required tests before handover or operation.

Safety is site-specific. Work must be planned and supervised by competent personnel in accordance with the applicable laws, standards, manufacturer instructions and approved method statements. This overview does not replace a formal risk assessment or site safety plan.

HELPFUL ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

A few starting points before planning a home elevator.

Can an elevator be added to an existing home? +

In many cases it may be possible, but feasibility depends on the building layout, available space, structural conditions and selected system. A site assessment is essential.

Do all home elevators require a pit or machine room? +

Requirements vary by model and OEM. Some systems may offer pitless or machine-room-less configurations; confirm details for the chosen equipment.

How much does a home elevator cost? +

Pricing depends on travel, stops, rated load, finishes, civil work, installation and service arrangements. Contact Orbix for a project-specific discussion.

How long does installation take? +

The timeline depends on site readiness, equipment lead time, civil work, installation and inspection requirements. A confirmed schedule should be agreed with the supplier.

What maintenance is required? +

Maintenance frequency and service coverage depend on the installed equipment and OEM guidance. Confirm the service agreement before purchase.
